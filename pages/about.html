About Dual Vision Events

Our Background

Dual Vision Events was started right here in Gqeberha. After years of dreaming about running my own business, I decided to build a company that helps people bring their events to life -- from small family gatherings to full weddings and functions. What began as an idea has grown into a hands-on equipment hire service for our community.

Our Vision

To be Gqeberha's most trusted event equipment hire company, known for reliable service and helping every client's event look exactly the way they imagined it.

Our Mission

To provide well maintained, affordable equipment and friendly, personal service so that our clients can focus on enjoying their event instead of worrying about the setup.
